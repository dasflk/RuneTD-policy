# Privacy Policy

[한국어](./privacy-ko.html)

Nara Games (Representative: JeongHyeon Park; "we", "us") operates the mobile game **Tower Battle** (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, and how it is used, stored and deleted.

- Effective date: November 1, 2026

---

## 1. Information We Collect

| Category | Data | When |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Apple account identifier (Sign in with Apple) or Google Play Games account identifier, game account ID (PlayFab ID) | When you sign in |
| Profile | Nickname | When you set a nickname |
| Gameplay | Stage progress, battle/co-op records and rankings, owned items (runes, skins, currencies), achievements | While you play |
| Purchases | Purchased products, transaction ID, receipt data | When you make an in-app purchase |
| Device & connection | IP address, device model, OS version, app version, app installation identifier (Unity), access time, error logs | Automatically while you use the app |
| Refund details (if applicable) | Bank account details for a refund (bank name, account number, account holder) | When we refund an Apple payment directly (for example, at service termination; Terms of Service Articles 6 and 9) |
| Support | Email address, device information (device model, OS version, app version, device identifier), game log files you choose to attach | When you send a bug report or contact us by email |

- Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Apple and Google. We never receive them.
- Sign in with Apple may share your name and email with us. We do not store them.
- We do not process sensitive data, national identification numbers or pseudonymized data, and we do not collect behavioral data for targeted advertising.

## 2. How We Use Information and Legal Basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating your account, signing you in and identifying you | Performance of a contract |
| Saving your progress, multiplayer matchmaking and rankings | Performance of a contract |
| Verifying in-app purchases, delivering items and handling refunds (including direct refunds) or disputes | Performance of a contract; compliance with legal obligations (Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce) |
| Preventing cheating and fraud | Our legitimate interests |
| Diagnosing errors, improving stability and analyzing usage statistics | Our legitimate interests |
| Responding to your inquiries | Performance of a contract at your request |

## 3. Retention

- We delete the data on our servers **without delay when you delete your account**.
- The following records are kept where the law requires it:

| Record | Period | Where it is kept | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payment and supply records (order number, payment date, product, amount) | 5 years | Kept by Apple and Google, which process the payment, under their own policies and applicable law | Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce |
| Consumer complaint and dispute records (support and refund emails) | 3 years | Our support mailbox (Gmail) | Same Act |
| Server processing and error logs (game account ID, request results) | Automatically deleted after 90 days | Microsoft Azure Application Insights | Service stability and fraud prevention |
| Usage statistics (by app installation identifier, not linked to your game account) | Under Unity's policies | Unity Technologies | Usage analytics |

- Refund bank account details are deleted without delay once the refund is completed.

## 4. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except when required by law (for example, a lawful request from an investigative authority).

## 5. Service Providers and International Transfers

We use the following providers to run the Game. Some data is stored on servers outside your country.

- When and how: data is transferred over the network whenever you use the Game.
- Retention: until you delete your account or the service contract ends (legally required records in Section 3 are kept for the periods stated there).
- Through each provider's terms and privacy commitments, we ensure that our providers protect your data at a level equivalent to this policy.

| Provider (country) | Purpose | Data | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Corporation – Azure PlayFab, Azure Functions and Storage (USA) | Accounts, sign-in, game data storage, rankings, server features | Account identifiers, nickname, gameplay data, purchase records, IP address | [privacy.microsoft.com](https://privacy.microsoft.com) |
| Exit Games GmbH – Photon (Germany; servers in the South Korea region) | Real-time multiplayer | Game account ID, nickname, IP address | hello@photonengine.com |
| Unity Technologies Inc. (USA) | Content delivery (CCD), in-app purchase processing, usage analytics | Device & connection data (including the app installation identifier), purchase records | DPO@unity3d.com |
| Apple Inc. (USA) | Sign in with Apple, App Store payments | Account identifier, purchase records | [apple.com/legal/privacy/contact](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/contact/) |
| Google LLC (USA) | Google Play Games sign-in, Google Play payments, receiving and storing support emails (Gmail) | Account identifier, purchase records, support emails (email address, device information, log files) | [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |

If you do not agree to these transfers, you can stop using the Game and request account deletion. Note that the Game cannot be used without them.

## 6. Deletion of Data

- Data is deleted without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled.
- Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

## 7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time.

- **Account deletion**: Menu ▸ Setting/Language ▸ Delete Account, or email us at the address below. See [How to Delete Your Account](./delete-account.html#how-to-delete-your-account) for details.
- When emailing, please include your in-game nickname, sign-in method (Apple / Google) and additional details that help us verify the account (such as when you last played, or an order number if you have made a purchase). If we cannot verify your account, we may ask you to delete it in the Game instead.
- We will process your request without delay (within 10 days at the latest).
- You may also exercise your rights through a legal guardian or an authorized agent. In that case, please include proof of the authorization.

## 8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

## 9. Security

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Minimal server access rights and managed access keys

## 10. Local Storage and Tracking

The Game does not use web cookies. It stores some data on your device (volume, language, deck settings, sign-in state), which is removed when you uninstall the app. The Game does not track you for advertising purposes and does not collect advertising identifiers.
