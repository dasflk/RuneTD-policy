# Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect

| Category | Data | When |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Apple account identifier (Sign in with Apple) or Google Play Games account identifier, game account ID (PlayFab ID) | When you sign in |
| Account (email sign-up) | Email address, password (stored encrypted) | When you sign up with email |
| Profile | Nickname | When you set a nickname |
| Gameplay | Stage progress, battle/co-op records and rankings, owned items (runes, skins, currencies), achievements | While you play |
| Purchases | Purchased products, transaction ID, receipt data | When you make an in-app purchase |
| Device & connection | IP address, device model, OS version, app version, access time, error logs | Automatically while you use the app |
| Support | Email address, game log files you choose to attach | When you send logs or contact us |

- Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Apple and Google. We never receive them.
- Sign in with Apple may share your name and email with us. We do not store them.

## 2. How We Use Information

- Creating your account, signing you in and identifying you
- Saving your progress, multiplayer matchmaking and rankings
- Verifying in-app purchases, delivering items and handling refunds or disputes
- Preventing cheating and fraud
- Diagnosing errors, improving stability and analyzing usage statistics
- Responding to your inquiries

## 3. Retention

- We delete your data **without delay when you delete your account**.
- Some records are kept longer where the law requires it:
  - Payment and supply records: 5 years (Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  - Consumer complaint and dispute records: 3 years (same Act)
  - Access (sign-in) logs: 3 months (Korean Protection of Communications Secrets Act)

## 4. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except when required by law (for example, a lawful request from an investigative authority).

## 5. Service Providers and International Transfers

We use the following providers to run the Game. Some data is stored on servers outside your country. Data is transferred over the network while you use the Game and kept until you delete your account or the service contract ends.

| Provider (country) | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Corporation – Azure PlayFab (USA) | Accounts, sign-in, game data storage, rankings, server features | Account identifiers, nickname, gameplay data, purchase records, IP address |
| Exit Games GmbH – Photon (Germany; servers in the South Korea region) | Real-time multiplayer | Game account ID, nickname, IP address |
| Unity Technologies Inc. (USA) | Content delivery (CCD), in-app purchase processing, usage analytics | Device & connection data, purchase records |
| Apple Inc. (USA) | Sign in with Apple, App Store payments | Account identifier, purchase records |
| Google LLC (USA) | Google Play Games sign-in, Google Play payments | Account identifier, purchase records |

If you do not agree to these transfers, you can stop using the Game and request account deletion. Note that the Game cannot be used without them.

## 6. Deletion of Data

- Data is deleted without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled.
- Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

## 7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time.

- **Account deletion**: [In-game Settings ▸ Delete Account], or email us at the address below.
- When emailing, please include your in-game nickname and sign-in method (Apple / Google) so we can verify your account.
- We will process your request without delay (within 10 days at the latest).

## 8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

## 9. Security

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Minimal server access rights and managed access keys
- Encrypted password storage

## 10. Local Storage and Tracking

The Game does not use web cookies. It stores some data on your device (volume, language, deck settings, sign-in state), which is removed when you uninstall the app. The Game does not track you for advertising purposes and does not collect advertising identifiers.
